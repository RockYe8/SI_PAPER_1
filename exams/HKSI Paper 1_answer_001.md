Question 31
Result: Correct Option: A
Explanation:
Under the FRR (Financial Resources Rules), "Liquid Assets" are calculated by applying a "haircut" (discount) to the firm's assets based on how quickly they can be converted to cash.
I. Fixed assets (like real estate/office property) cannot be quickly converted to cash, so they receive a 100% haircut (meaning they contribute 0 to liquid assets).
II. Unsecured loans also receive a 100% haircut because there is no collateral to guarantee recovery.
III. Free cash in a bank account is fully liquid, receiving a 0% haircut (counted at 100% value).
IV. Listed shares (like HSI constituents) are relatively liquid but have market risk, so they receive a specific partial haircut (e.g., 15% or 20% depending on the specific rule schedule, but distinctly not 100% or 0%).
Source: Study Manual Chapter 4, Section 2 (Capital Requirements - FRR).
