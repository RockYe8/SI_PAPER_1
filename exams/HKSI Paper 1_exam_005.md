## Question 5 (Topic: Topic 2: Principles of relevant Hong Kong law and the Companies Ordinance)
English:

Jerry states, "This note is fully backed by sovereign bonds and has zero risk of principal loss." Jerry honestly believed this because he skimmed a marketing brochure, but he did not read the prospectus which clearly stated that the principal is at risk if the issuer defaults. Sarah invests HK$500,000 and subsequently loses 80% of her investment when the issuer defaults. 
Which of the following statements regarding Jerry's liability and the legal classification of his statement is CORRECT?

A. Jerry’s statement is classified as an innocent misrepresentation because he honestly believed it, and therefore Sarah has no legal remedy under the Misrepresentation Ordinance.
B. Jerry’s statement is classified as a negligent misrepresentation because, although he believed it, he lacked reasonable grounds for his belief as he failed to read the prospectus. Sarah can seek rescission of the contract and/or damages.
C. Jerry's statement is classified as a fraudulent misrepresentation because he acted recklessly. Fraudulent misrepresentation is the only category of misrepresentation that allows for damages in Hong Kong.
D. Jerry's statement is considered an "invitation to treat" rather than a representation, meaning no contractual liability can arise from it.

香港傳統中文:
Jerry 是一間持牌法團的持牌代表，他向客戶 Sarah 推薦了一隻結構性票據。Jerry 聲稱：「這隻票據完全由主權債券支持，本金損失風險為零。」Jerry 確實對此信以為真，因為他粗略瀏覽了一份宣傳單張，但他並沒有閱讀招股說明書，而招股書中清楚載明若發行人違約，本金將面臨風險。Sarah 投資了 50 萬港元，隨後因發行人違約而損失了 80% 的投資金額。
關於 Jerry 的法律責任以及其陳述的法律分類，下列哪項敘述是正確的？

A. Jerry 的陳述被歸類為無意圖的失實陳述，因為他真誠地相信該陳述，因此根據《失實陳述條例》，Sarah 沒有法律救濟途徑。
B. Jerry 的陳述被歸類為疏忽的失實陳述，因為雖然他相信該陳述，但他缺乏合理的依據，因為他未能閱讀招股說明書。Sarah 可以尋求撤銷合約及／或索取損害賠償。
C. Jerry 的陳述被歸類為欺詐的失實陳述，因為他的行為十分魯莽。在香港，欺詐的失實陳述是唯一允許索取損害賠償的失實陳述類別。
D. Jerry 的陳述被視為「要約邀請」而非陳述，這意味著不會因此產生任何合約責任。
