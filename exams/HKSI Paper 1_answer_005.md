Explanation:
English explanation:

- Statement II is INCORRECT: Under equity, a fiduciary must account for any secret profit obtained from their position, regardless of whether the principal suffered a financial loss or if the price was fair.
- Statement III is CORRECT: Section 9 of the Prevention of Bribery Ordinance (Cap. 201) prohibits an agent from soliciting or accepting an advantage without the permission of their principal. Both the giver (seller) and receiver (agent) can be prosecuted.
- Statement IV is CORRECT: Secret profits made by a fiduciary in breach of duty are deemed to be held on "constructive trust" for the principal. Sarah can claim them.

中文解释：

- 敘述 II 錯誤：根據衡平法，受託人必須交代因其身份而獲得的任何秘密利潤，無論委託人是否遭受財務損失，或交易價格是否公平。
- 敘述 III 正確：《防止賄賂條例》（第 201 章）第 9 條禁止代理人在未獲得其委託人許可的情況下索取或接受利益。提供者（賣家）和接受者（代理人）均可能被起訴。
- 敘述 IV 正確：受託人違反義務所賺取的秘密利潤，被視為以「歸入信託」方式為委託人持有。Sarah 有權向其追討。

## Question 9
Result: Correct Option: A

Explanation:
English explanation:
- The Investor Compensation Fund (ICF) limit is HK$500,000 per claimant per default in respect of securities traded on the SEHK.
- The compensation limit is applied on a *per-investor* (per-claimant) basis, rather than a *per-account* basis.
- In a joint account, each joint account holder is treated as having an equal share of the account (unless there is contrary evidence) and can claim up to their individual limit of HK$500,000, which aggregates with their sole accounts at the same defaulted broker.
- Let's analyze the claims:
  - Mr. Cheung's loss: HK$400,000 (sole account) + HK$400,000 (his 50% share of the joint account) = HK$800,000 total loss. His claim is capped at the maximum limit of HK$500,000.
  - Mrs. Cheung's loss: HK$600,000 (sole account) + HK$400,000 (her 50% share of the joint account) = HK$1,000,000 total loss. Her claim is capped at the maximum limit of HK$500,000.
- Therefore, the maximum aggregate compensation they can claim is: HK$500,000 (Mr. Cheung) + HK$500,000 (Mrs. Cheung) = HK$1,000,000. Option A is correct.

中文解释：
- 對於在聯交所交易的證券，投資者賠償基金（賠償基金）的賠償上限為每名申索人因單一違約事件而獲得的 50 萬港元。
- 賠償上限是按「每名投資者」（每名申索人）計算，而非按「每個帳戶」計算。
- 對於聯名帳戶，每名聯名帳戶持有人被視為擁有該帳戶的平等份額（除非有相反證據），且其個人的賠償上限為 50 萬港元。此額度會與其在同一家违约經紀商開立的個人帳戶合併計算。
- 讓我們分析申索金額：
  - 張先生的損失：40 萬港元（個人帳戶）+ 40 萬港元（聯名帳戶的 50% 份額）= 80 萬港元總損失。其申索金額受限於 50 萬港元的最高上限。

Source: HKSI Paper 1 Study Manual, Chapter 3, Section "Investor Compensation - Compensation Limits and Joint Accounts".

## Question 10
Result: Correct Option: B

Explanation:
English explanation:
- Under Part XV of the SFO (Disclosure of Interests), there are different rules for "Substantial Shareholders" (5% threshold) and "Directors/Chief Executives" of listed corporations.
- For directors, the disclosure threshold is ANY interest (no 5% minimum threshold applies). Therefore, Mr. Lee must disclose his initial 2.5% holding upon appointment. Statement I is incorrect.

- Directors must disclose ANY short position they hold, with no minimum threshold (the 1% short position threshold only applies to non-director substantial shareholders). Thus, Mr. Lee must disclose his 0.5% short position within 3 business days of 15 June. Statement IV is incorrect.
- Therefore, only Statements II and III are correct, which corresponds to Option B.

中文解释：
- 根據《證券及期貨條例》第 XV 部（權益披露），上市公司的「大股東」（5% 門檻）與「董事／最高行政人員」適用不同的規則。
- 對於董事而言，披露門檻是「任何」權益（不適用 5% 的最低門檻）。因此，李先生在被委任時必須披露其初始持有的 2.5% 權益。敘述 I 錯誤。

## Question 11
Result: Correct Option: A

Explanation:
English explanation:
- Statement I is CORRECT: Under the SFO, the civil (MMT) and criminal routes for market misconduct are mutually exclusive. Once the SFC initiates MMT proceedings or the Secretary for Justice initiates criminal prosecution, the other route is barred to prevent double jeopardy.
- Statement II is INCORRECT: Because the routes are mutually exclusive, a concluded MMT proceeding bars any subsequent criminal prosecution for the same conduct, regardless of new evidence.
- Statement III is CORRECT: MMT proceedings are civil in nature and use the civil standard of proof (balance of probabilities), whereas criminal prosecutions in courts must be proven beyond a reasonable doubt.
- Statement IV is INCORRECT: The MMT does NOT have the power to impose prison sentences (it can only impose civil sanctions like disqualification, cold shoulder, disgorgement, and fines up to HK$10 million). Prison sentences (up to 10 years) can only be handed down by criminal courts.

中文解释：
- 敘述 I 正確：根據《證券及期貨條例》，市場失當行為的民事（審裁處）和刑事途徑是互斥的。一旦證監會發起審裁處程序，或律政司司長提起刑事檢控，另一條途徑即被封鎖，以防雙重起訴。
- 敘述 II 錯誤：由於兩條途徑是互斥的，已終結的審裁處程序會封鎖隨後針對相同行為的任何刑事檢控，不論是否發現新證據。
- 敘述 三 正確：審裁處程序屬民事性質，使用民事舉證準則（衡平概率），而法院的刑事檢控則必須達到合理疑點之外的舉證準則。
- 敘述 IV 錯誤：審裁處沒有權力判處監禁（它只能施加民事處分，如取消資格令、冷淡對待令、歸還利潤令及高達 1000 萬港元的罰款）。監禁刑罰（最高達 10 年）只能由刑事法院判處。

Source: HKSI Paper 1 Study Manual, Chapter 3, Section "Market Misconduct Tribunal & Dual-Route System" and Chapter 7.
