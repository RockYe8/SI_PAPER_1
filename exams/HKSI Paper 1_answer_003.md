## Question 7
Result: Correct Option: A
Explanation:
English explanation:
- Scenario I constitutes naked short selling and is a criminal offence under SFO Section 170. A verbal promise from a friend does not give Mr. Tang a "presently exercisable and unconditional right" to vest the securities at the time the order is placed.
- Scenario II does NOT constitute naked short selling. A formal, legally binding stock borrowing agreement gives Mr. Tang a "presently exercisable and unconditional right" to vest the securities, even if physical delivery to his account is pending.

中文解释：

Source: Chapter 7, Section 3.26, 3.27, 3.31 & 3.35

## Question 11
Result: Correct Option: B
Explanation:
English explanation:
- Statement I is correct: Common law and the HKSI manual explicitly identify the broker-client, company director-company, and solicitor-client relationships as Fiduciary Relationships (受信關係) [1.16, 1.16].
- Statement II is correct: A fiduciary owes a duty of loyalty and must not make a secret personal profit from their position without the fully informed consent of their principal. It is irrelevant if they acted in good faith or if the principal suffered no loss; the rule is applied strictly [1.16, 2.42].
- Statement III is correct: SFO and Companies Ordinance allow companies to apply for civil remedies such as rescinding a contract or seeking an account of profits (disgorgement of secret profits) if a director fails to disclose their material interests [2.45].
- Statement IV is incorrect: Any ratification of a director's breach of fiduciary duty (such as negligence, default, or breach of trust) must be passed by a resolution of the company's members, but the votes of the director concerned, their connected entities, and those holding shares on trust for them MUST be disregarded (not taken into account) under Section 2.46 of Chapter 2 [2.46].
Therefore, statements I, II, and III are correct.

中文解释：
- 陳述 I 正確：普通法及溫習手冊明確指出，股票經紀與客戶、公司董事與公司，以及律師與客戶之間的關係屬於「受信關係」 [1.16, 1.16]。
